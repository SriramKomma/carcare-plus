CarCare+

Find real repair shops and EV chargers in Ontario
Roadside help for Ontario drivers

What's wrong with your car?

Pick the problem from the list. CarCare+ works out which service you need and finds the nearest shops that can fix it.

    How it works

    1

    Pick the problem

    Choose what's wrong from the list, like "Car won't start" or "Flat tire", and add details if you like.

    2

    Find shops nearby

    CarCare+ matches the problem to a service and lists the closest real shops.

    3

    Contact in one tap

    Call, email or get directions straight from the result.

    Live nearby

    Live results from Google Maps with ratings, reviews and opening hours. These are fetched for each search and not stored.

    Or

    Course tools

    The data structures and algorithms behind the search, each one you can try on the real pages.

    Word completion

    A Trie of every word in the pages. The most frequent words come first.

    Spell check

    Levenshtein edit distance against the vocabulary from the text files. Up to 1 edit for words of 4 letters or fewer, otherwise 2.

    Edit-distance table

    The dynamic-programming matrix. Highlighted cells show one cheapest path.

    Word frequency on a page

    Counts come from the inverted index. Leave the word empty to see the page's most frequent words.

    Search history

    How often each word has been searched (saved in data/search-history.txt).
